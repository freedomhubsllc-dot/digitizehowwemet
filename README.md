# DigitizeHowWeMet.com

Official landing page and checkout portal for **DigitizeHowWeMet.com**, a DBA under **Freedom Hubs LLC** (Freedom Hubs LLC DBA Digitize HWM Services).

## Overview
DigitizeHowWeMet.com provides specialized video preservation, tape digitization, and AI photo animation services tailored for weddings, rehearsal dinners, anniversaries, and family heritage archives in the Greater Houston / North Corridor area.

## Key Service Offerings

### Wedding Packages
- **Package 1: Our Signature Rehearsal Film ($250)**: Up to 5-minute custom film, up to 6 VHS tapes + 14 animated photos (or 20 photos), client-provided script, 100 QR guest favor cards.
- **Package 2: The Heritage Archive ($400)**: Package 1 plus all full-length raw converted tape MP4s, individual animated photo files, and custom branded flash drive.

### Add-On Rates
- **Extra Minutes**: $19.99 / minute
- **Extra Tape Conversion**: $39.99 / tape
- **Extra Photo Animation**: $4.99 / photo
- **Additional Favor Cards**: $100.00 / 50 cards

### General Non-Wedding Services
- **Tape Conversion Tiers**: 1–4 tapes ($39.99/ea), 5–10 tapes ($34.99/ea), 10+ tapes ($29.99/ea)
- **Photo Animation Tiers**: 1–5 photos ($4.99/ea), 5–10 photos ($3.99/ea), 10+ photos ($2.99/ea)
- **5-Min Photo Montage**: $50 flat for 20 photos + client script ($2.99/extra photo)
